The PureGlow Skincare Ritual

How to maximize your skin wellness and extend the lifespan of your handcrafted cold-process soap bars.

The Daily 3-Step Ritual

01

Prep & Warm Wetting

Splash warm water onto your face and body. The gentle steam opens skin pores, prepping them to release toxins and fully absorb raw organic nutrients.

02

Double Lather Massage

Rub the soap bar between your palms to generate a thick, creamy botanical lather. Apply gently in slow circular upward motions, letting it sit for 30 seconds to work its herbal magic.

03

Cold Rinse & Seal

Rinse completely with fresh cool water. The thermal shift seals skin pores, locking in natural botanical plant glycerin and herbal hydration. Pat dry with a soft bamboo towel.

Artisan Soap Care Mastery

Handmade cold-process soaps are packed with natural glycerin (a humectant that pulls moisture from the air). Because they contain zero chemical hardening agents, they require a little extra love to last longer.

Use a Slotted Dish

Always keep your soap on a well-draining wooden or ceramic slotted dish. Soap bars left sitting in water pools melt quickly.

Keep Away from Spray

Store the soap outside the direct spray of the shower. Allowing the bar to dry completely between uses doubles its life.

The Half-Bar Strategy

Cut your 100g bar in half using a clean knife. Use one half in the shower, and keep the other dry in a cool drawer. This maximizes overall lifespan.
